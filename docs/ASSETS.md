### Prompt da edição do retrato

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: portrait for the introduction/about section of Fernanda Lemos's nutritionist website. Input image 1 is the exact portrait to EDIT; input image 2 is only a STYLE REFERENCE for her existing brand symbol. Create ONE subtly branded version of the portrait, at the same portrait aspect ratio. Preserve the photographed woman with maximum identity fidelity: exact facial structure, eyes, teeth, smile, skin texture and freckles, age, hair color and hairstyle, hand supporting chin, pose, body proportions, white coat and beige top, seated position and table. Do not beautify, reshape, retouch away wrinkles, replace the person, change her expression, add jewelry, or alter the foreground subject. Preserve the original warm natural lighting, ivory wall, leaf shadows, blurred greenery at the left, and the warm chair and table. ONLY add a very delicate small-to-medium fine line illustration in the clear wall space at the LEFT of her, beside her shoulder and hand, without crossing her face, hair or body: two gently outlined organic leaves in muted sage/olive joined to one light dusty blush curved stem with a subtle DNA-inspired twist, directly inspired by the CENTER SYMBOL in image 2. The motif should look like a tasteful quiet editorial line ornament, comparable to an understated outline heart over a lifestyle photo; airy hairline strokes, modest scale, enough visibility on the cream wall but low prominence, no filled shapes, no sticker or badge. No brand name or any text, no circular lettering, no watermarks, no border. Do NOT add a literal heart since her brand is leaves. Keep the portrait the main focus. Keep the original crop, especially all of her head and both arms visible. Background stays realistic and soft, with just a very slight warm blush finish. Output one finished high-quality portrait.
