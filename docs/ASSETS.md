# Origem dos assets

## Marca fornecida

Originais preservados em `assets/brand/logotipo-original.png` e `assets/brand/sublogo-original.png`. Os arquivos enviados não foram alterados.

`assets/img/logo.webp` e `assets/img/seal.webp` são recortes dos limites úteis dos arquivos transparentes enviados, convertidos sem perda. Ícones derivam do símbolo original; não houve redesenho da marca. O logotipo original é usado no cabeçalho, apresentação e rodapé. A capa social é uma composição de marca, título e fotografia para compartilhamento.

## Fotografias criadas para este projeto

Duas imagens foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, no modo de criação. Não representam uma paciente, a profissional, seu consultório ou um plano alimentar prescrito. São composições editoriais ilustrativas.

Arquivos entregues:

- `assets/img/hero-480.webp`, `hero-800.webp` e `hero-1024.webp`.
- `assets/img/ritual-640.webp`, `ritual-1200.webp` e `ritual-1536.webp`.
- `assets/img/social-cover.jpg`: capa social derivada da fotografia e da marca.

Versões WebP responsivas foram produzidas para servir somente o tamanho necessário. As fotografias não dependem de serviços externos nem de hotlink.

### Prompt da fotografia principal

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial hero photography for a premium Brazilian nutritionist website, Fernanda Lemos. Create one exceptionally beautiful photorealistic still-life, portrait 1024x1536 composition. A warm pale ivory travertine table in soft Mediterranean afternoon sunlight, viewed from a gently elevated angle. A large handmade matte cream ceramic shallow bowl in the lower center with a naturally composed fresh meal: roasted pumpkin wedges, chickpeas, quinoa, avocado slices, baby green leaves, thin radish slices, a drizzle of olive oil, no rigid segmented diet portions. On the upper right an understated small olive branch in a sculptural pale clay vase, linen napkin in dusty pale blush below left, a cut lemon near the bowl, a glass of water behind, cropped brushed brass fork. Muted olive, ivory, blush pink and natural warm terracotta color world. Realistic organic food texture, tactile ceramic, soft film grain, dappled leaf shadows on wall/table, high-end slow-living magazine photography, art directed yet relaxed. Bowl occupies middle 55 percent of composition, generous clear space at top for responsive cropping. Not a collage. NO typography, text, logos, branding, watermarks, people, faces, hands, medical equipment, weighing scales, pills. Mood: calm, human, nourishing, abundant, never clinical. Save image as project asset.

### Prompt da fotografia de apoio

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a supporting full-width photograph for an elegant Brazilian nutritionist's website. Landscape 1536x1024, high-end editorial still-life from a very slight overhead angle: a rustic warm ivory table set simply for a slow everyday breakfast, a linen cloth in pale blush draped loosely at bottom left, a small handmade cream plate with two slices of sourdough bread, a small ceramic bowl of papaya and berries, a pear, a cut orange, a modest ceramic cup of coffee, a glass carafe of water. Olive foliage enters subtly at one edge. Composition has an intentional open area on the left 35 percent, food gently clustered in center and right. Clear bright sunlight with beautiful soft leaf shadows, authentic tactile details, not glossy, filmic, natural imperfect styling. Warm ivory, muted olive green, pale terracotta and dusty rose. Image communicates that nourishment is part of life and pleasure, not restrictive dieting. No text, no logo, no watermark, no people or body parts. Photographic, not illustration. Calm and understated.
